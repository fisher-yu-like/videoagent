# Station meeting — controlled three-shot experiment

Two recurring actors meet on one railway platform. Actor A wears orange and enters from screen-left. Actor B wears blue and waits on screen-right. The sequence preserves left-to-right movement and keeps the camera on the north side of the action axis.

1. `s01`, 5 seconds, wide establishing shot. The camera makes a small truck-right move. Actor A walks from x=-3 to x=-1; actor B waits at x=1.5.
2. `s02`, 5 seconds, medium shot. The camera dollies toward the actors. Actor A walks from x=-1 to x=0.5; actor B remains at x=1.5.
3. `s03`, 5 seconds, over-shoulder shot. The camera makes a small clockwise arc while both actors remain in place and face each other.

This is a deliberately controlled synthetic story used to test executable camera and actor blocking. It is not real-world footage and is not evidence of a video-generation model's controllability.
